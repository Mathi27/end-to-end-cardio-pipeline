📱 Product Feature List: Cardio-AI Assistant
1. Core Diagnostic Dashboard
Patient Data Input Form: A clean, user-friendly sidebar or form to input the 13 clinical attributes (Age, Sex, CP, Cholesterol, etc.).

Real-time Risk Gauge: A speedometer-style chart that moves instantly between "Healthy" (Green) and "High Risk" (Red) as data is entered.

Confidence Score Indicator: A distinct badge showing how certain the model is (e.g., "85% Confidence").

2. Interactive Analysis Tools
"What-If" Risk Simulator:

Functionality: Sliders for modifiable factors (Cholesterol, Max Heart Rate, Blood Pressure).

User Value: Allows doctors to show patients, "If you lower your cholesterol by 20 points, your risk drops by 15%."

Cohort Comparison (Radar Chart):

Functionality: Overlays the current patient's vitals against the "Average Healthy Patient" of the same age/gender.

User Value: Instantly visualizes which specific metrics are abnormal (e.g., "Everything is normal except Oldpeak").

3. Explainability & Trust (XAI)
SHAP Waterfall Chart:

Functionality: A graph showing exactly why the model made its prediction (e.g., "+10% risk due to Asymptomatic Chest Pain", "-5% risk due to good Heart Rate").

User Value: Moves the product from a "Black Box" to a clinically trusted tool.

Population Scatter Plot ("You Are Here"):

Functionality: Plots the patient as a glowing dot on a graph of the entire 920-patient dataset.

User Value: Shows if the patient sits in a cluster of healthy or sick historical cases.

4. Utility & Reporting
One-Click Medical Report (PDF):

Functionality: Auto-generates a branded PDF summary with the Patient's ID, Risk Score, Key Risk Factors, and standard medical recommendations.

User Value: Saves documentation time for GPs.

📊 Dataset Visualization capabilities
These are specific charts you can build using the 14 attributes:

Age vs. Risk Distribution: Histogram showing the "danger zone" ages.

Chest Pain Type Correlation: Bar chart correlating CP Types (1-4) with Disease presence.

Max Heart Rate vs. Disease: Box plot proving that lower max heart rates correlate with disease.
